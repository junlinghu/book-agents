# Harbor Jar — store policy

This file is the source of truth for returns, shipping, damage, and discounts.
Hours, allergens, and gift boxes live in `faq.md`. Quote this file for the rules below.

## Returns

- Unopened jars, bottles, and sealed gift boxes may be returned within 14 days of delivery.
- A paper or email receipt is required.
- Refunds are store credit only. Harbor Jar does not give cash refunds.
- Opened jars are final sale. Once the seal is broken, the jar cannot come back.
- Opened oils, opened spice tins, and tasted preserves are final sale for the same reason.

## Shipping

- Shelf-stable oils, preserves, spices, and gift boxes ship inside the United States, including Ohio.
- Fresh labneh does not ship. Fresh labneh is pickup only.
- The catalog row is the source for stock and for the shippable flag. This page does not list counts.
- Orders under $40 have a $7.00 shipping fee. Orders of $40 or more ship free inside the United States.
- Harbor Jar does not ship internationally, to PO boxes, or to freight forwarders.
- Orders are packed within 2 business days. Nothing ships on Sunday, Monday, or Tuesday, when the store is closed for packing.

## Damage in transit

- Email care@harborjar.example within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of the damaged jar and the order number.
- Harbor Jar replaces the damaged jar once, or refunds that jar as store credit.
- The shipping fee is not refunded.

## Discounts

- A discount code is not applied in chat. It waits for a person.
- This page does not list a percent off.
- A note on a gift box is not a discount.
- The seller does not invent a code and does not change a catalog price.
