# Harbor Jar — FAQ

Returns, shipping, damage, and discounts are only in `policy.md`.
This file does not restate those rules.

## Hours and pickup

- The online store takes orders any day.
- Pickup is Wednesday–Saturday, 11:00–17:00, at 18 Wharf Street, Port Meridian.
- The store is closed Sunday, Monday, and Tuesday for packing. There is no pickup on those days.
- A customer who asks for hours should hear the pickup window and the closed days.

## Allergens

- Sesame crunch contains sesame.
- Fig and thyme jam is figs, sugar, and thyme. It is packed on a line that also packs sesame crunch, so it may contain traces of sesame.
- Calabrian chili oil is chili and olive oil. The jar itself has no sesame.
- Harissa is chili, cumin, and coriander.
- The kitchen is not a dedicated allergen-free room. Do not promise a sesame-free order.
- An allergy on a customer's preference file applies to that customer. It does not rewrite the catalog.

## Gift boxes

- The Harbor gift box is a fixed assortment: chili oil, fig jam, and harissa.
- Jars inside a sealed box cannot be swapped.
- A gift note can be added at checkout. A gift note is not a discount and not a custom box.
- The price and the stock live in the catalog, not in this paragraph.
