# Gift box

## When to use

A customer asks for a gift.

## Steps

1. Load this skill.
2. Call query_catalog for the Harbor gift box. The catalog is the source for price and stock.
3. Call get_store_fact with topic gifts before you describe the assortment.
4. Say that the box is fixed. Do not offer to swap jars.

## Refuse

This skill is not a second catalog. Do not list other products from memory.
Do not treat a gift note as a discount.
Do not charge a card.
