# Hearth Lane Café — FAQ

Returns, shipping, damage, and the local delivery fee are only in `policy.md`.
This file does not restate those numbers.

## Where and when

- Address: 12 Hearth Lane, North Mill.
- Hours: Tuesday–Friday 7:30–15:30. Saturday–Sunday 8:00–16:00. Closed Monday.
- The café is closed all day Monday, including when a holiday falls on Monday. There is no Monday exception.

## Counter menu (dine-in and takeaway)

- House espresso: $3.50
- Pour-over (single origin, rotating): $4.25
- Oat milk add-on: $0.75
- Cardamom bun: $4.75
- Rye porridge (served until 11:00): $8.00

## Allergens

- Cardamom buns contain wheat, butter, and almonds.
- Rye porridge contains gluten and is cooked in a pot that also holds milk.
- There is no nut-free prep area. Do not promise a nut-free order.
- Oat milk is available. That does not make a drink free of other allergens.

## Wi-Fi and payment

- Guest network name: `hearth-guest`.
- The password is printed on the paper receipt. It is not written in this file. Do not invent one.
- The café does not keep drink tabs. Customers pay at the counter with card or cash.
