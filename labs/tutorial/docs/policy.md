# Hearth Lane Café — counter policy

This file is the source of truth for returns, shipping, damage, and local delivery.
Hours, address, and menu prices live in `faq.md`. Quote this file for the rules below.

## Returns

- Unopened retail coffee (bag valve seal intact) may be returned within 14 days of purchase.
- A paper or email receipt is required.
- Refunds are store credit on a Hearth card only. The café does not give cash refunds.
- Opened coffee, ground coffee, pastries, drinks, and any food that has left the counter are final sale.
- Hearth cards (gift cards) are single-use and not returnable.
- Equipment sold at the counter (kettles, filters, scales) may be exchanged within 30 days with a receipt if it is unused and in the original box. Opened equipment is exchanged for the same item only when it is defective.

## Shipping

- The café does not ship pastries, drinks, milk, or anything that needs refrigeration.
- Whole-bean and ground coffee ships inside the United States only.
- Coffee orders are packed within 2 business days. Nothing ships on Monday, when the café is closed.
- The shipping fee on coffee orders under $40 is $6.00. Coffee orders of $40 or more ship free.
- The café does not ship internationally, to PO boxes, or to freight forwarders.

## Damage in transit

- Email counter@hearthlane.example within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of the damaged bag and the order number.
- The café replaces the damaged item once, or refunds that item as Hearth card credit.
- The shipping fee is not refunded.

## Local delivery

- Bike delivery covers addresses within 3 miles of 12 Hearth Lane, North Mill.
- The delivery fee is $4.50. Orders of $35 or more are delivered free.
- The delivery window is Tuesday–Friday, 8:00–14:00. There is no local delivery on Saturday, Sunday, or Monday.
- Delivery is sealed coffee and same-day pastry boxes. Hot drinks are not delivered.
